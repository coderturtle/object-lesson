# Skeptical Practitioner / Critic — critique of Object Lesson design docs

**1. The entire differentiator rests on an unhedged capability claim: that Coachgremlin can reliably tell real encapsulation from cosmetic encapsulation.** The doc hedges the *auxiliary* claim ("a deterministic first gate should make Coachgremlin's job easier and more trustworthy... **Working hypothesis, not a proven finding**") but never hedges the underlying claim that an LLM grader *can do this judgment task at all*. Instead it asserts obligation: "Coachgremlin's conceptual tier exists precisely to catch this, **and must**, since the deterministic tier structurally cannot" (What you keep, Module 03). Whether an agent reliably distinguishes "an invariant the class actually protects" from a getter/setter pasted on for show is exactly the kind of unproven claim this doc hedges everywhere else — here it's stated as settled.

**2. "Every track" overclaim from two data points.** "a second, independent confirmation that **this is how Exercism structures every track**, not a Rust-specific practice" — generalizing to Exercism's entire catalog from two tracks (Rust, Java) checked across two workshops is a bigger claim than the evidence supports.

**3. Inconsistent evidentiary standard inside the differentiator claim itself.** The positive curriculum-structure claims are sourced to direct fetches ("fetched and confirmed directly"). But the negative claims used to justify the differentiator — "**none** teach agent-native, **none** grade a design-judgment conceptual tier beyond 'does it compile'... **none** leave a keepable takeaway artifact beyond the solved exercise" — get no equivalent citation or fetch, despite being stated with the same flat confidence, right before the doc itself calls the overall bet "genuinely untested."

**4. An unhedged population assumption, explicitly walled off from scrutiny.** "That gap — subject-beginner, harness-fluent — is the whole design constraint, **not a risk to flag or soften**." Whether a first-year with zero OOP background but genuine daily coding-agent fluency is a real, findable cohort (vs. hypothetical) is precisely the kind of claim this doc's own house style would normally hedge — here it's declared out of bounds for hedging.

No issue found with the Checkstyle caveat itself — it's unusually honest and specific about what it won't catch.
